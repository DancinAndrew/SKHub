# Resume health check

For an audit-only request, review before rewriting. If rewriting is already requested, apply the findings directly.

Check positioning and first-scan clarity, then inspect each meaningful bullet for actual action, method, scope/result, and relevance to the target role. Distinguish measured outcomes, scoped benchmarks, defensible estimates, scope counts, and unsupported precision. Preserve an estimate when its basis and wording are honest; do not upgrade prototypes or student work into production ownership.

Choose section order based on the candidate and target; Education/Summary/Experience/Projects/Skills is an option, not a universal mandate. Include projects when they add evidence not already covered. Group skills by capability and connect them to demonstrated work.

For customer-facing engineering roles, look for discovery, demos/POCs, integration, troubleshooting, stakeholder communication, feedback, and documentation. A missing signal remains a gap, not permission to invent experience.

Lead with the few issues that materially affect credibility or fit and the smallest correction. Use bullet/metric tables only when they help inspect a long resume. Preserve settled wording and defensible numbers; ask only for missing information that prevents an accurate requested rewrite.
